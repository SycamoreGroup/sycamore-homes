Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is an HCS provider?+
HCS stands for Home and Community-based Services, a Texas Medicaid waiver program supporting adults with intellectual and developmental disabilities in residential and community settings rather than institutional care.
How does the placement process work?+
We start with an intake consultation to understand medical needs, behavioral considerations, and daily routines. From there, our team coordinates with guardians and service coordinators to match an appropriate, sustainable placement.
What areas do you serve?+
We operate group homes in Arlington, Dallas, Denton, Houston, and Corpus Christi, with our business office based in Houston, TX.
Do you offer support outside residential homes?+
Yes. Our Individual Support Services (ISS) provide flexible in-home, on-site, or off-site care for people who don't need full residential placement.
How do I get started?+
Request an intake consultation below, or call us directly at (832) 335-4098. We'll help you choose the meeting type that fits where you are in the process.

Stay Involved

Care works best as a partnership
